About ExoMy

ExoMy is a growing Malaysian exotic pet community bringing together hobbyists, breeders, keepers, rescuers, educators, and animal lovers from all backgrounds. Built on the values of responsibility, ethics, knowledge, and respect for animal welfare, ExoMy promotes safer and more educated exotic pet keeping culture in Malaysia. From reptiles and amphibians to birds, arachnids, mammals, aquatic species, and invertebrates, ExoMy celebrates the beauty and diversity of exotic animals while encouraging proper care and responsible ownership.

Education

Verified species information, care guides, awareness posters, and husbandry knowledge for both beginners and experienced keepers.

Community

Building a supportive Malaysian exotic pet community based on positivity, inclusiveness, passion, and mutual respect.

Responsibility

Promoting ethical ownership, legal awareness, animal welfare, and safe hobby practices for the future of the community.

Exotic Species

Reptiles

Snakes, geckos, tortoises, monitors, skinks, chameleons, and many more.

Amphibians

Frogs, salamanders, axolotls, and unique aquatic species.

Birds

Parrots, exotic birds, aviary species, and tropical companions.

Arachnids

Tarantulas, scorpions, jumping spiders, and fascinating invertebrates.

Mammals

Sugar gliders, hedgehogs, ferrets, and other unique companion animals.

Aquatics

Rare fish, shrimp, aquatic ecosystems, and exotic aquatic habitats.

ExoMy Mission

ExoMy believes education creates better keepers, healthier animals, and stronger communities. Our mission is to inspire Malaysians to enjoy the exotic pet hobby responsibly while respecting animal welfare, conservation, legality, and ethical keeping standards. Different Pets, One Passion, Strong Together.
